    Who we are

    Opening paragraph

    An all-volunteer rescue in Springtown, Texas, placing Border Collies and Border Collie mixes across Central and North Texas.

    Body text

    We pull dogs from shelters, owner surrenders and the occasional hoarding case. Every dog is vetted, heartworm tested, spayed or neutered, and fostered in a real home before being placed.

    🔒 The Five Freedoms, position statements and the euthanasia policy are locked. Changing those is a board decision, so they need an administrator rather than an editor.
